Privacy policy

Home Assistant Calendar connects one person's Google Calendar to their own Home Assistant server. This page explains what happens to that data.

Data accessed

Google Calendar events and calendar names for the signed-in Google account, through the Google Calendar API.

How it is used

Events are shown on a display in the owner's home and can be used by the owner's own home automations. Nothing else.

Where it is stored

Only on the owner's Home Assistant server on their home network, including the sign-in token Google issues. No copy is kept anywhere else.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine-learning models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections, or by removing the Google Calendar integration in Home Assistant, which deletes the stored token.
